# Athena Production Planning

Version: 1.0

---

## Purpose

Production Planning transforms validated intelligence into an executable production roadmap.

Athena does not simply recommend content.

Athena decides what the organization should produce, in what order, and why.

The objective is to maximize business impact while minimizing unnecessary production effort.

Production Planning converts intelligence into prioritized work.


---

## Production Queue

Every recommendation enters a Production Queue.

The queue represents Athena's recommended order of execution.

Each Production Asset receives:

- Priority
- Strategic Objective
- Supporting Evidence
- Expected Business Impact
- Estimated Effort
- Dependencies
- Target Audience
- CTA
- Approval Status

The Production Queue is dynamic.

As new market signals appear, priorities may change.


---

## Production Assets

A Production Asset is any deliverable created by the organization.

Examples:

- Instagram Carousel
- Facebook Post
- LinkedIn Article
- Reel
- YouTube Video
- Podcast Episode
- Webinar
- PDF Guide
- White Paper
- Landing Page
- Email Campaign
- Sales Script
- FAQ
- Case Study
- Knowledge Base Article

Production Assets are implementation outputs.

They are not intelligence.


---

## Dependencies

Production Assets often depend on other assets.

Example:

Career Change Guide

↓

Carousel promoting guide

↓

Email campaign

↓

Webinar

↓

Sales follow-up

Athena should recommend dependency-aware production planning rather than isolated content ideas.


---

## Production ROI

Athena should estimate expected impact before production begins.

Examples:

- Lead generation
- Engagement
- Authority building
- SEO value
- Community growth
- Sales enablement
- Appointment generation

Production recommendations should always be tied to measurable business objectives.
