# Athena Intelligence Profiles

Version: 1.0

---

## Purpose

Athena Core is universal.

Intelligence Profiles make Athena specialized.

Rather than changing Athena itself, every business loads its own Intelligence Profile.

An Intelligence Profile represents everything Athena must know about a specific business, organization, or client in order to reason correctly.

Examples:

- GetOblic
- Elevate / Aluma
- ENB
- TattooMD
- Future Enterprise Clients

Athena Core never changes.

Only the active Intelligence Profile changes.

---

## Intelligence Profile Structure

Each profile contains several independent components.

### 1. Business Identity

Examples:

- Company name
- Industry
- Products
- Services
- Mission
- Positioning
- Target market

---

### 2. Strategy Pack

Commercial methodology.

Examples:

- Offer hierarchy
- Qualification methodology
- Sales philosophy
- CTA strategy
- Objection handling
- Buyer psychology

---

### 3. Knowledge Base

Long-lived institutional knowledge.

Examples:

- FAQs
- Internal documentation
- Training material
- Case studies
- Methodologies
- Research
- Compliance information

---

### 4. Brand Voice

Defines communication.

Examples:

- Tone
- Vocabulary
- Reading level
- Professionalism
- Writing style
- Executive style

---

### 5. Business Rules

Operational constraints.

Examples:

- Never promise outcomes
- Regulatory compliance
- Escalation rules
- Pricing policy
- Refund policy
- Approval policy

---

### 6. AI Configuration

Model-level configuration.

Examples:

- Preferred model
- Temperature
- Confidence thresholds
- Required citations
- JSON schemas
- Prompt overrides


---

## Decision DNA

Decision DNA defines how an organization makes decisions.

It is not the same as brand voice or sales strategy.

Decision DNA influences how Athena prioritizes, recommends, and evaluates actions.

Examples:

### GetOblic

- Move fast
- Test continuously
- Prefer automation
- Prioritize ROI
- Ship MVPs
- Use data over intuition
- Optimize for scalability

### Elevate / Aluma

- Education before selling
- Authority before promotion
- Trust before conversion
- Premium positioning
- Human transformation stories
- Relationship-first growth

### ENB

- Evidence-first
- Compliance-aware
- Patient advocacy
- High empathy
- Precision
- Insurance-navigation expertise

Decision DNA should affect:

- Opportunity prioritization
- CTA recommendation
- Reply direction
- Content Intelligence
- Campaign recommendations
- Risk assessment
- Executive Briefings
