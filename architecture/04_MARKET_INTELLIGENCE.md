# Athena Market Intelligence

Version: 1.0

---

## Purpose

Market Intelligence transforms Community Intelligence into cross-market strategic knowledge.

Community Intelligence answers:

"What is happening inside this community?"

Market Intelligence answers:

"What is happening across the market?"

Market Intelligence should identify recurring patterns, emerging trends, strategic risks, and opportunities across every monitored community.

Its purpose is to help executives understand market evolution rather than individual conversations.


---

## Market Signals

The smallest unit of Market Intelligence is a Market Signal.

A Market Signal represents an observed pattern that appears across multiple discussions or communities.

Examples:

- Growing demand
- Emerging objection
- Pricing concern
- Career-transition interest
- Certification confusion
- Technology adoption
- Competitive mention
- Regulatory concern

A signal is not an opinion.

A signal must be supported by observed evidence.


---

## Signal Lifecycle

Every Market Signal progresses through a lifecycle.

Observed

↓

Repeated

↓

Validated

↓

Trending

↓

Strategic

↓

Resolved

Signals should strengthen or weaken over time based on new evidence.

Signals may eventually disappear when market conditions change.


---

## Evidence Chain

Every Market Signal must be traceable.

Athena should never recommend strategic action without supporting evidence.

Each recommendation should be linked to the observations that produced it.

Example:

Discussion

↓

Discussion Intelligence

↓

Community Intelligence

↓

Market Signal

↓

Market Intelligence

↓

Production Intelligence

↓

Executive Recommendation

The complete chain should remain available for audit and executive review.


---

## Confidence

Confidence represents Athena's certainty that a Market Signal accurately reflects market reality.

Confidence should never depend solely on LLM certainty.

Instead, confidence should consider:

- Number of supporting discussions
- Number of supporting communities
- Evidence consistency
- Time persistence
- Signal stability
- Human validation

Confidence should evolve as additional evidence is collected.


---

## Executive Outputs

Market Intelligence should answer questions such as:

- What changed this week?
- What pain points are increasing?
- Which buyer stages are growing?
- Which objections are disappearing?
- What opportunities deserve attention?
- Which communities produce the highest-quality discussions?
- What should the business produce next?
- Which strategic risks require executive attention?

Market Intelligence should present evidence-backed insights rather than operational details.
